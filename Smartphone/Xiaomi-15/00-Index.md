## ⚡ Top 5 High-Impact (No-Root)

1. **Animation scales → 0.5x** · Developer Options · snappiest feel
2. **MSA disable** · `pm disable com.miui.msa.global` · kills ads system-wide
3. **80% charge limit** · Settings → Battery → Battery Health → Charging Limit
4. **Refresh rate → Auto** · Settings → Display → LTPO efficiency
5. **Analytics off** · `pm disable com.miui.analytics` + `com.xiaomi.ab`
